Delete your Trace account

Deleting your Trace account permanently removes your account and everything stored with it — every saved diagnosis and generated circuit, and your credit balance. It cannot be undone.

From inside the app

  1. Open Trace and sign in.
  2. Go to About.
  3. Tap Delete my account, type DELETE to confirm, and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Google Play account (Subscriptions) or your App Store account first, or billing continues for an account that no longer exists.

What is deleted

  • Your account and Google-derived profile (email, name, picture).
  • Every saved debugging session and generated circuit.
  • Your credit balance and any unlimited-plan entitlement.
  • Your sign-in sessions.
  • The purchase records held against your account identifier.

What is not stored in the first place

Photographs sent for diagnosis are never written to our database. They are transmitted for a single request, analysed, and discarded — so there is nothing of that kind to delete.

What may be retained

Anonymised transaction records, where Apple or Google require them for refunds and accounting. These are no longer associated with you.
