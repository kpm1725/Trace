Privacy Policy

Trace, by Violet Seed Labs · Last updated: TODO — set before publishing

Trace is an AI-assisted circuit debugging app. This policy describes what it collects, what it does with it, and what it does not do. It is written to match how the app actually behaves; if you find a discrepancy, the discrepancy is a bug and we would like to hear about it.

What we collect

  • Your Google account identity — email address, name, and profile picture, received when you sign in with Google. We use it to identify your account and nothing else. We never receive your Google password.
  • What you send for analysis — the symptom text and any notes you type, and the plain-English descriptions you write for circuit generation.
  • What the app produces — the diagnoses and circuits generated for you, saved to your history so you can reopen them.
  • Purchase records — which credit packs or subscriptions you bought, so your balance can be credited. Payment card details are handled entirely by Apple or Google and never reach us.
Photographs are not stored. A photo you send for diagnosis is transmitted for that one request, analysed, and discarded. It is not written to our database and is not kept in your history — the saved diagnosis contains the model's written description of what it saw, not the image.

Who else sees it

Three processors, each doing one job:

  • Anthropic receives the photo, symptom, or description for each request, in order to produce the response. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Google handles sign-in, and Google Play handles purchases on Android.
  • RevenueCat processes in-app purchases and tells our server what to credit. It receives your Trace account identifier so the purchase reaches the right account.

We do not sell your data, we do not share it for advertising, and there are no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

Where it is stored

Account data and saved sessions live in MongoDB Atlas; the API runs on Railway. Data is transmitted over TLS.

How long we keep it

Your account and its saved sessions are kept until you delete them. You can delete an individual session at any time from its detail screen, and deleting your account removes everything at once. Expired sign-in sessions are discarded automatically. Anonymised purchase records may be retained where the app stores require it for refunds and accounting.

Deleting your account

In the app: About → Delete my account. It asks you to type DELETE to confirm, then removes your account and all of its data immediately and permanently.

If you cannot open the app, see the account deletion page.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel that in your Google Play or App Store account, or billing continues.

Your rights

You can access your data through the app, delete it as described above, and ask us any question about it at the contact address below. If you are in the UK, EU, or a jurisdiction with comparable law, you also have the right to request a copy of your data and to object to processing — write to us and we will action it.

Children

Trace is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Safety

Trace's diagnoses are generated by an AI model from a photograph and a description. They are suggestions, not professional advice, and the app states plainly what a photograph cannot determine. You are responsible for your own safety when working on a circuit — particularly anything connected to mains voltage, or any battery chemistry capable of a thermal event.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how your data is used, notify you in the app.
